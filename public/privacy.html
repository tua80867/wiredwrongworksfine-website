Last updated: October 27, 2026

Wired Wrong, Works Fine operates a private YouTube analytics utility used by the owner of the Wired Wrong, Works Fine YouTube channel.

Google and YouTube Data

The utility may request read-only access to YouTube channel and analytics data through Google OAuth. This access is used only to retrieve information for the owner's own YouTube channel and organize that information in a private Google spreadsheet for personal analysis and channel management.

Data Use

Data accessed through Google or YouTube APIs is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties. The utility is not offered as a public analytics service.

Data Storage

Retrieved channel and analytics information may be stored in the owner's private Google Sheets and related Google account resources. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored using Google Apps Script properties or other private account storage and are not intentionally made public.

Data Sharing

No Google user data obtained through this utility is sold or transferred to third parties except as necessary to operate the Google and YouTube services used by the utility, or when required by law.

Revoking Access

Google account access may be revoked at any time through the Google Account security and permissions settings.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to wiredwrongworksfine@duck.com.
