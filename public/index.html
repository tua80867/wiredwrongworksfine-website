The idea behind Wired Wrong

Curiosity is the starting point.
Making is how we learn.

Wired Wrong, Works Fine explores how things work by building, testing, modifying, repairing, and improving real projects.

From a first circuit to a custom 3D-printed part, the goal is to turn an idea into something you can hold, test, and make better.
