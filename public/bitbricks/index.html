Bit Bricks Publisher

A private channel-management application operated by Timothy Jared Boger for the Bit Bricks YouTube channel.

What the app does

Bit Bricks Publisher prepares pixel-art guessing videos and uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, apply thumbnails, manage video metadata and playlists, schedule approved releases, and read and manage channel comments. It also retrieves channel and video information to check posting coverage and maintain local operational records.

Google authorization is used only for the operator's connected YouTube channel. The publishing dashboard runs locally on the operator's computer; this website provides public app information and policies. The app is not offered as a public sign-in or publishing service.

Permissions and your data

The application requests YouTube upload, read-only and channel-management permissions needed for these functions. Some tasks use owner-directed AI assistance for editorial drafting and quality review. See the privacy policy for the data involved, storage, service providers and deletion choices.
