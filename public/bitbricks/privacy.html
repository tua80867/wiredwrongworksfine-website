Privacy policy

App: Bit Bricks Publisher
Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy covers Bit Bricks Publisher, a private application operated by Wired Wrong, Works Fine for the Bit Bricks YouTube channel. The separate Wired Wrong, Works Fine analytics utility has its own privacy policy.

Google and YouTube information accessed

With the operator's Google authorization, the app accesses channel identifiers, video identifiers, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, privacy and scheduling information, processing status, playlist membership, and comment threads. Comment records may include public author display names, channel identifiers, text, timestamps and reply information.

The app can upload videos, update video metadata and thumbnails, manage playlists, schedule releases, and post or manage authorized channel comments. It uses the YouTube Data API. It does not request Gmail, Google Contacts or Google Drive permissions through this uploader connection.

How information is used

Information is used to operate the connected channel: prepare and review publishing packages, prevent duplicate uploads and comments, verify release settings and covers, plan posting coverage, review viewer feedback, and diagnose operational failures. Access is limited to these channel-management purposes.

Storage and security

Operational data is stored in local files, reports and planning spreadsheets on the operator's Windows computer. The working directory may be synchronized through the operator's OneDrive account, and selected project records may be included in the operator's private GitHub backup.

The uploader's OAuth credentials are stored separately using Windows account-bound encryption. Credentials and authorization tokens are not published on this website or intentionally included in public repositories or AI prompts. Access remains subject to the security of the operator's devices, accounts and connected providers.

Service providers and AI-assisted processing

Google and YouTube receive API requests needed to carry out the authorized actions. Microsoft OneDrive and GitHub may process stored project records for synchronization and private backup. OpenAI Codex may process selected channel/video metadata, recent channel-authored comments, and owner-selected comment context when the operator invokes AI-assisted drafting or review. Finished video frames, artwork and audio evidence may also be supplied for quality review. This assistance is used for this application's channel-management tasks; it is not a feature for training general-purpose AI models.

Google and YouTube data is not sold or rented, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated parties. Disclosure may also occur when required by law. The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Publishing receipts, review evidence, comment-task history and operational records are retained to support ongoing channel operation, troubleshooting and duplicate prevention until the operator removes them. This includes copies in synchronized storage and backups. The app does not currently provide an automatic deletion schedule or a public self-service deletion interface.

To request deletion of stored information, contact wiredwrongworksfine@duck.com and identify the affected channel, video or comment. The operator can remove relevant local records and coordinate removal of synchronized and backup copies. Deleting local app records does not itself delete videos or comments already on YouTube.

Revoking Google access

The operator can revoke the app's authorization at any time through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorized API access but does not automatically erase previously stored app records or content already published on YouTube. Use the deletion contact above for stored records and YouTube's controls for published content.

Website and third-party services

These public information pages do not offer Google sign-in or collect Google credentials. The website is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes requests to serve and secure the site. Links to Google, YouTube and other services are governed by those services' own policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact and updates

Contact Wired Wrong, Works Fine at wiredwrongworksfine@duck.com with privacy questions or deletion requests. Changes to these practices will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
