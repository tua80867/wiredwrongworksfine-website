Can You Build a Music Jewelry Box That Plays When It Opens?

A music jewelry box is a simple idea, but making it play automatically when the lid opens turns it into a real product-design problem. In this video, I combine Snap Circuits, 3D printing, a Melody IC, a tilt-triggered circuit, and USB-C power to build an interactive box that plays music when opened.
